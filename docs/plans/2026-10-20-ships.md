# Two ships: the classic rocket and the Explorer

## Goal

Next to the original rocket, a pilot can fly the **Explorer**: a ship from a concept art the user
brought in (made with Gemini). It should look as close to that picture as possible, with all of its
parts:
- an egg-shaped hull, white with panel lines and rivets;
- a navy nose with a cyan glowing seam;
- a big glass visor with the cockpit behind it, set in a silver, navy and orange frame with cyan
  lights;
- a glowing cyan line down its side;
- vents, a sensor and a side thruster on its right;
- an open engine bay over a copper bell;
- four swept landing legs, white and navy, tipped orange.

## Choosing

- Each pilot has a `ship` (`'classic' | 'explorer'`, in their profile).
- **The hangar** (`Hangar.tsx`) opens when the game starts for a pilot who hasn't chosen yet:
  - a new pilot, after the welcome;
  - a pilot from before there was a choice, who is told a new ship has landed.
  It shows both ships side by side, with the pilot's animal in each window. They tap one and fly
  it.
- **To change later:** ✏️ next to the pilot's name has a "Change ship" button, which opens the
  hangar again (now with a ✕).
- The game waits while the hangar is open.

## The Explorer's front

- **The picture.** The concept art itself, cut out of its background (`scripts/explorer-ship.py`
  → `public/ships/explorer.webp`, about 100 KB). The cut is made where the dark outline encloses the
  ship, a hair inside it, so no light fringe shows against the sky.
- **Placement.** It's laid into the rocket's 200 × 400 frame at the classic's own scale:
  - its bell's bottom where the classic's nozzle is (so the 3D plume comes out of it);
  - its visor at the height of the classic's porthole, and about its size.
- **The window.** The ship is seen three-quarters on, so the visor is left of the centre line. The
  rocket now turns about its window wherever it is, and the camera's dive (tap the window three
  times for the Academy), the cockpit's fly-in and the window's shimmer all follow it
  (`flight.porthole`).
- **The pilot.** The pilot's animal sits in the cockpit's seat, behind a cyan tint of glass.
- **Admirals.** A Space Admiral's Explorer has its orange trim turned gold (a second picture made
  by the same script) and wears the admiral's medal under the visor.
- **Where it shows.** The 3D rocket (its texture twice the classic's size, for the detail), the
  wormhole's dive, and the hangar.
- **Crews.** A crew flight keeps the classic rocket with its two windows: it's everyone's.

## The Explorer's back

The rounds that fly behind the pilot's ship (Fleet Battle, Stranded Fleet, Acceleration Road,
Space Traffic, the Black Sun, the arrivals) draw its back in the same picture-book way as the
classic's (`explorerRearSvg`):
- the egg of a hull, with the navy nose beyond and its cyan seam;
- the visor's dome on top, where the pilot looks out (the same spot as the classic's canopy);
- the cyan line and navy panel down its side;
- the copper bell glowing in its engine bay;
- the four legs splayed out, tipped orange (gold, with a gold star on the nose, for an admiral).

It has one engine where the classic has two, so the engine glows, flames and sparks come from the
bell. Other ships in those rounds (a fleet, teammates in Monster Sun) are still the classic, in
their colours.
