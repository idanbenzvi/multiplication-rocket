# Three ships: the classic rocket, the Explorer and Nova

## Goal

Next to the original rocket, a pilot can fly the **Explorer**: a ship from a concept art the user
brought in (made with Gemini). It should look as close to that picture as possible, with all of its
parts:
- an egg-shaped hull, white with panel lines and rivets;
- a navy nose with a cyan glowing seam;
- a big glass visor with the cockpit behind it, set in a silver, navy and orange frame with cyan
  lights;
- a glowing cyan line down its side;
- vents, a sensor and a side thruster on its right;
- an open engine bay over a copper bell;
- four swept landing legs, white and navy, tipped orange.

Then **Nova**, from a second concept art: lilac and rose gold, with mint-glowing light lines, a
cream panel scrolled with rose-gold filigree round a great round window (the cockpit's seat and
screens behind it), rose-gold collars and vents, an open rose-gold engine bay (pipes and a little
gear) over its bell, and four legs curving out like petals, striped cream and mint.

## Choosing

- Each pilot has a `ship` (`'classic' | 'explorer'`, in their profile).
- **The hangar** (`Hangar.tsx`) opens when the game starts for a pilot who hasn't chosen yet:
  - a new pilot, after the welcome;
  - a pilot from before there was a choice, who is told a new ship has landed.
  It shows both ships side by side, with the pilot's animal in each window. They tap one and fly
  it.
- **To change later:** ✏️ next to the pilot's name has a "Change ship" button, which opens the
  hangar again (now with a ✕).
- **A new ship** opens the hangar once more for every pilot who hasn't seen it (`shipsSeen`, the
  number of ships the hangar had when they chose; a pilot who chose before Nova saw two). Their
  own ship is already ticked, and each ship they haven't seen is marked *New!*.
- The game waits while the hangar is open.

## The pictured ships' fronts

- **The picture.** The concept art itself, cut out of its background (`scripts/ship-cutout.py
  explorer|nova <art>` → `public/ships/<ship>.webp`, about 100 KB each). The cut is made where the dark outline encloses the
  ship, a hair inside it, so no light fringe shows against the sky.
- **Placement.** It's laid into the rocket's 200 × 400 frame with its window at the height of the
  classic's porthole and its bell's bottom on the centre line:
  - the Explorer at the classic's own scale, its bell where the classic's nozzle is;
  - Nova at the scale that makes her window the Explorer's size. She stands taller, so her nozzle
    is lower in the frame, and each ship says where its nozzle is, for the 3D plume to come out
    of it.
- **The window.** The ships are seen three-quarters on, so the window is off the centre line: left
  of it on the Explorer, right of it on Nova. The
  rocket now turns about its window wherever it is, and the camera's dive (tap the window three
  times for the Academy), the cockpit's fly-in and the window's shimmer all follow it
  (`flight.porthole`).
- **The pilot.** The pilot's animal sits in the cockpit's seat (right of the Explorer's middle,
  left of Nova's), behind a tint of the glass's colour.
- **Admirals.** A Space Admiral's ship has its trim turned gold (the Explorer's orange, Nova's rose
  gold: a second picture made by the same script, leaving what's behind the glass alone) and wears
  the admiral's medal under the window.
- **Where it shows.** The 3D rocket (its texture twice the classic's size, for the detail), the
  wormhole's dive, and the hangar.
- **Crews.** A crew flight keeps the classic rocket with its two windows: it's everyone's.

## The pictured ships' backs

The rounds that fly behind the pilot's ship (Fleet Battle, Stranded Fleet, Acceleration Road,
Space Traffic, the Black Sun, the arrivals) draw its back in the same picture-book way as the
classic's (`explorerRearSvg`):
- the egg of a hull, with the navy nose beyond and its cyan seam;
- the visor's dome on top, where the pilot looks out (the same spot as the classic's canopy);
- the cyan line and navy panel down its side;
- the copper bell glowing in its engine bay;
- the four legs splayed out, tipped orange (gold, with a gold star on the nose, for an admiral).

Nova's back (`novaRearSvg`) is a lilac egg with its paler nose and a mint ring beyond, a rose-gold
collar over a mint line, mint lines down its sides, the window's dome in its rose-gold frame, the
rose-gold engine bay glowing, and its four legs curving out, striped cream and mint and tipped rose
gold.

Each has one engine where the classic has two, so the engine glows, flames and sparks come from the
bell. Other ships in those rounds (a fleet, teammates in Monster Sun) are still the classic, in
their colours.
