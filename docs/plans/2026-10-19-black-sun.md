# The Black Sun: the final boss

## Goal

A finale worth working towards. Once a child has **mastered every fact** of their mission (each
one right at least 4 times out of 5, as on the practice map: 55 facts on ×, 60 on +), the monster
sun comes back as a black hole that swallows numbers. Curing it takes **15 right answers in a
row**, proof that they really know the table, and makes them a **Space Admiral**: *Master of
Universal Multiples*.

## Rules

- **Unlock:** `bossReady(mastery, mission)`: every fact of the mission mastered. The first time
  that's seen, the pilot's record keeps it open for good (a fact slipping back under 80% later,
  in the boss itself say, doesn't lock it again). Until then the card in 🎮 Game modes is dimmed
  and shows `have / need`. New and not yet opened: a dot on 🎮, a NEW tag, *It has appeared!*,
  and the Grand Master badge's toast points the way.
- **The run:** 15 right in a row cure it; the Black Sun's life is what's left of the 15.
- **Checkpoints** every 5 in a row (5 and 10).
  - A miss **before** the first checkpoint: the sun swallows the run (back to 0; it gets that
    life back).
  - A miss **after** a checkpoint: the sun throws a **fireball**. Dodged: the run carries on.
    Hit: the run falls back to the last checkpoint (it gets back what was above it).
  - The `n`th fireball of a fight takes `max(950, 1750 − 160·(n − 1))` ms to arrive, after a
    0.95 s wind-up (input during the wind-up counts).
- **No skipping a fact you don't know:** a missed fact comes back after 2 other questions, and
  keeps coming back till it's answered right.
- **Questions:** on ×, facts 2–10, weighted by the pilot's own due score (their weak facts) ×
  how tough the fact is (the middle of the table most, ×2 and ×10 least), nothing asked in the
  last 8; once the run is 10 or more, only 3–9. On +, sums from the whole mission (two-digit sums
  included), tracked as the mission tracks them. Four choices, the usual near misses; the arrow
  cross on a computer, 2 × 2 on touch.
- Every answer goes into the pilot's mastery map and answer log (source `boss`).
- **Winning** is saved at once (so leaving mid-finale still counts): the record's win count, the
  `spaceAdmiral` badge (no toast: the finale shows it) and with it the **Space Admiral** rank,
  above Honored Captain, reachable from any rank.

## The show

- **Arrival:** Monster Sun's (`arrival/eclipse.ts`, extended, not copied): out of the warp, the
  ship flies in at the eclipse, the corona flares, the diamond ring flashes, the eyes open and
  follow it. The ship stays, below the sun. The rules card comes in at the same moment as Monster
  Sun's lobby (`ARRIVAL_MS`), and the camera eases the sun over to make room for it.
- **Black hole:** the corona is sucked round (`drawCorona`'s swirl) and tinted dark, a whirlpool
  spins up behind it (a band, three spiral arms winding in, streaks of matter streaming round and
  in, Doppler-bright on the near side) and a ring of light hugs the rim. Numbers spawn all round
  and spiral in on the same plane, faster and stretched thin as they near the dark; one is
  swallowed the moment it touches the disc on screen (so none disappears behind it) with a star
  blast: a flash, a four-pointed sparkle, a ring and sparks pulled back in.
- **A right answer:** the number lifts off its button to the ship, which kicks back and fires a
  bolt of light; it lands on the lower rim (flash, sparks, shockwave, a wince) and opens the next
  of 15 cracks of light in the shell (made at the start, each branching off the ones before).
- **A miss:** the number is pulled off its button on a timed spiral into the hole; the sun's eyes
  narrow and its jagged mouth opens to laugh. A heal seals the cracks above the run and pulls a
  red ring in.
- **Checkpoint:** shards of the shell burst off, a gold shockwave, the shield in the life bar
  breaks for good.
- **Fireball:** eyes blazing, a furnace in the mouth with the fireball forming in it; then it
  comes at the ship, growing, trailing embers, the screen's edges reddening. A dodge is a barrel
  roll out of the way, held until it has gone past (it flies on over where the ship was); a hit
  is an explosion, the ship tumbling, a shake.
- **The cure** (seconds from the 15th bolt): it shakes, cracks blazing, eyes wide (0–0.45);
  the shell shatters, a white flash, shockwaves, and every number it swallowed bursts back out
  (0.45); the ship rolls (0.9), loops (1.4) and flies in close beside it, smaller (2.6–3.8),
  while the camera closes in (1.2–3.6); the gold core spreads out from the middle and the face
  turns happy (1.5); rays grow (1.7), three dead little worlds bloom blue and green one by one
  (2.4, 2.95, 3.5), a rainbow arcs over it (2.7), hearts float up; the admiral's card at 4.7 with
  a fanfare and fireworks. The whole mode plays its own soundtrack, *The View from the Last
  Lap*.
- **The badge** (`AdmiralBadge.tsx`, SVG): a gold medal on a burst of rays, in a laurel wreath,
  the cured sun smiling under five stars, a ribbon. In the finale its rays turn and a shine sweeps
  it; in the badges screen it's the badge and the Space Admiral insignia.

## Architecture

- `src/game/blackSun.ts`: the rules, pure and tested (`blackSun.test.ts`): `answerBoss`,
  `dodgeFireball`, `nextBossQuestion`, `fireballFlightMs`, `bossProgress`, `bossReady`.
- `src/storage/bossRecord.ts`: per pilot and mission: unlocked, seen, wins, best run. Cleared by a
  progress reset (for that mission) and when the pilot is deleted.
- `src/game/useBossStore.ts`: opening and closing (the game paused underneath, like the other
  modes, the soundtracks), the fight, recording answers, saving the win.
- `src/components/boss/blackSunScene.ts`: `BlackSunScene extends EclipseScene`, on one 2D canvas
  (the ship too, so the fireball and explosions layer round it). It has its own clock, stepped by
  `useArrivalCanvas` (frozen while the player is away), and the overlay times its moves by it
  (`after`), so a fireball waits when the child leaves the tab. Sprites (numbers, the ship, glows)
  are drawn once and stamped.
- `src/components/boss/BlackSun.tsx`: the overlay: starfield, canvas, the life bar, the slot below
  (or beside, on a wide screen) for the rules, the question and the dodge buttons, banners, and the
  admiral's card. It measures the free part of the screen for the scene to fit the sun into. Its
  keys are taken in the capture phase and stopped, so the game paused underneath never sees them
  (and `QuestionForm` now ignores keys while the game is paused).
- `arrival/eclipse.ts` changed only to share: `drawCorona` pulled out (with a swirl, 0 by
  default) and the helpers made protected; Monster Sun's arrival draws exactly as before
  (compared frame by frame).
- Dev mode: Shift+X opens it, Shift+G / Shift+J answer right / wrong, Shift+Y jumps to one short
  of the cure.

## Testing

- Unit tests for the rules: the run, checkpoints, swallowing, fireballs dodged and taken, retries,
  the question mix, the unlock.
- Playwright on a faked clock, frame by frame through the arrival, a bolt, a swallow, a
  checkpoint, a dodge, a hit and the cure: phone upright and on its side, tablet in Hebrew,
  computer with the arrow keys, reduced motion; and in real time, a whole fight won by tapping, the
  card's states, the badges screen. 60 fps throughout on the production build with the CPU slowed
  4× at a pixel ratio of 2.
