# The mixed mission: × and + together

## Goal

A third mission next to Multiplication and Addition, for a child who knows a bit of both and
should keep both fresh, switching between them as they go.

## Rules

- **Its own journey** (the user's choice): levels, fuel, streaks, the practice map and the badges'
  progress are kept for it on their own, like each mission's (`progress:v1:<id>:mix`). Switching to
  it starts at level 1, with the sums at stage 1.
- **Each question is × or + at random** (the user's choice), half and half: `pickOp('mix')`.
  The × facts come from the whole 1–10 table, weighted by the pilot's own misses, as on ×; the sums
  from the pilot's addition stage, which opens the next stage on a launch once 80% of it is
  mastered, as on + (with one in five reviewing the earlier stages).
- **Bonus rounds** are each built around one operation, so a round picks one at its start
  (`bonusOp`, read by the rounds through `roundOp()`), and keeps it.
- **Mastery** is one map with both kinds of key ("7x8", "3+4", the two-digit kinds): 115 facts in
  all. The practice map shows it as two grids, a tab each.
- **The Black Sun** opens once all 115 are mastered; its questions are either kind.

## Around the game

- A fact's key still says which operation it is (`missionOfKey`): the hints, the wrong-answer
  explainers and the answer choices follow the question, not the pilot.
- `Mission` stays the type of an operation (`'mul' | 'add'`); a pilot's mission is
  `PilotMission` (`… | 'mix'`), with `opsOf`, `covers` and `pickOp` (`game/mission.ts`).
- **Recording:** in the game modes (Monster Sun, Split Sky, the Black Sun) and Crew Flight, an
  answer goes into the pilot's progress if their mission covers its fact (`covers`), so a mixed
  pilot's go into their mixed progress.
- **The answer log** now notes the mission each answer was given in (rows from before don't, and go
  by their fact's key), so the parent dashboard's three pills each show their own history, and a
  reset clears just that mission's.
- Crew Flight: a mixed pilot's turns are either kind (as a navigator, groups of stars or two
  groups together); docking is an addition round if there's an addition pilot aboard, at the stage
  of whoever doing sums is further back. Monster Sun: a mixed pilot can be sent either kind of
  missed fact to help with. Split Sky: a mixed host's game is either kind.
- The Deep Space Academy (the porthole): the multiplication one or the addition one, for the kind
  of question on screen.

## Testing

- `mixed.test.ts`: the operations it covers, the random pick, about half of each, sums at its
  stage, the 115-fact map, the Black Sun's unlock and questions.
- In the browser: a mixed pilot's questions, logo and banner, answers saved only under the mixed
  progress with log rows tagged, the map's tabs and the peek, every bonus round twice (each picking
  its own operation), the pilot picker.
